Course Summary

Five Areas I Studied

Data communication explains how information moves from one device to another through signals, hardware, software, standards, and secure network practices.

01

Networking Fundamentals

I studied LAN, WAN, MAN, network devices, IP addresses, bandwidth, topology, and how computers communicate through shared rules.

02

OSI and TCP/IP Models

The OSI model has seven layers, while TCP/IP uses four practical layers to explain how data is prepared, addressed, transmitted, and received.

03

Transmission Media

Guided media such as twisted pair, coaxial, and fiber optic cables carry signals physically. Unguided media use radio waves, microwaves, infrared, and satellites.

04

Signals and Data

Analog and digital signals can be affected by noise, attenuation, distortion, delay, and limited bandwidth, so modulation and encoding are important.

05

Security and Reliability

Firewalls, encryption, authentication, backups, and careful network design help protect data and keep communication services available.

How Communication Works

From Message To Receiver

A communication system needs a sender, message, transmission medium, receiver, and protocol. The protocol controls formatting, timing, error checking, addressing, and delivery.

  • LAN connects devices within a limited area.
  • WAN connects networks across cities, countries, and continents.
  • The internet links many networks using TCP/IP.
  • Satellites and wireless links support long-distance communication.
